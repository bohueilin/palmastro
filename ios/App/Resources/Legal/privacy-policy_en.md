# PalmAstro Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 9, 2026**

PalmAstro is a privacy-first self-reflection app. It analyzes photos of your palm together with tropical astrology signals — entirely on your device — to generate explainable, growth-oriented reports. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it stays, and how you control it.

## 1. The short version

- All analysis happens **on your device**. Your palm images, birthday, journal, and reports are never uploaded anywhere.
- We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.
- The app makes **no network requests**. Hand detection uses Apple's Vision framework, built into iOS and running on your device, so nothing needs to be downloaded.
- Raw palm photos are automatically deleted within 24 hours by default, and you can turn photo retention off entirely.
- You can permanently erase everything from the app at any time in Settings.

## 2. Data processed on your device

To provide its features, the app processes the following data. All of it stays on your device:

- **Palm photos**, captured with your camera during a scan.
- **Palm feature summaries** derived from those photos (for example, line contrast and continuity values). These are pattern metrics used for reflection — they are **not** used to identify you and are not biometric identification data.
- **Birthday**, and optionally **birth time and birth place**, used for astrology calculations performed locally.
- **Journal entries** you write.
- **Generated reports and scores**, plus your preferences (language, tone, dominant hand, reminders, retention setting).
- **A random install identifier**, generated on your device, used only to organize local data. It is never transmitted and is replaced with a new one whenever you delete all data.

## 3. What we do not do

- No account, sign-up, email, or phone number is required or requested.
- No personal data is transmitted off your device — not palm images, not palm features, not your birthday, not your journal, not your reports.
- No analytics or usage data is sent to us or to anyone else. The app currently transmits no analytics or crash reports at all. If we ever enable privacy-safe diagnostics in a future version, we will update this policy first and the data will never include palm images, palm features, journal text, birth details, or report contents.
- No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

## 4. Network access

The app does not connect to the internet. Hand detection runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework, which is built into iOS — no model download or other network request is needed. The app transmits nothing, to us or to anyone else.

## 5. Storage and security

- App data is stored in the app's private container and encrypted at rest with iOS Data Protection (complete protection), tied to your device passcode.
- The random install identifier is protected by the iOS Keychain and is not accessible to other apps.
- Palm photos are stored only in the app's private storage.
- Nothing is stored in shared locations, and nothing is uploaded to any cloud service.

## 6. Retention

- **Raw palm photos:** deleted automatically within 24 hours of capture by default. You can turn off photo retention in Settings, in which case scan photos are not kept after analysis.
- **Everything else** (profile, reports, journal, settings) stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## 7. Deleting your data

Settings → "Delete all my data" permanently erases, on your device:

- Your profile (birthday, birth time/place, hand, tone, and other preferences),
- All scans, palm photos, and derived palm features,
- All reports, scores, and history,
- All journal entries,
- All cached content, share images, and app settings.

The install identifier is also deleted and replaced with a new random one, and the app returns to its first-run state. Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app likewise removes all app data from your device.

## 8. Notifications

Monthly rescan reminders are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

## 9. Children

PalmAstro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, no data from children is collected either.

## 10. Your rights and control

Because all data stays on your device under your control, you can view, change, and erase it directly in the app at any time — no request to us is needed. If you have questions about your privacy rights under laws that apply to you (such as the GDPR or Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act), contact us and we will help.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data — for example, if optional diagnostics are ever introduced — we will update this policy, change the effective date above, and note the change in the app before the new behavior takes effect.

## 12. Contact

Questions or requests: **support@palmastro.app**

PalmAstro is a self-reflection tool. It does not provide medical, financial, or legal advice, and it does not predict the future.
