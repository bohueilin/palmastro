## Description — en-US

```
PalmAstro is a privacy-first self-reflection app. It uses on-device palm feature analysis and tropical astrology signals to generate explainable personal growth reports across four areas of life: career, wealth habits, family communication, and stress & recovery patterns.

PalmAstro is not fortune telling. It doesn't tell your future, diagnose your health, or advise your finances. It turns patterns into prompts: things to notice, one action for today, one for the week, and a question worth journaling about.

UNDERSTAND YOUR READING
• Each month's reading distills into simple guidance: what to lean into right now, and what to be mindful of.
• "Be mindful of" means gentle awareness, never a warning — the app doesn't predict misfortune, it points at habits worth watching.
• Every guidance item links back to the signals behind it, so you always know why it's there.

HOW IT WORKS
• A guided seven-angle palm scan with live quality feedback.
• On-device analysis extracts categorical line features (clarity, continuity, depth) — never a biometric identity template, and never uploaded.
• Your birthday (plus optional birth time and place) adds tropical astrology signals, computed locally.
• A deterministic rules engine scores each domain 0–100 with a grade and a confidence level.

EXPLAINABLE, NOT MYSTICAL
• Every score has a "How was this calculated?" breakdown of each signal's contribution.
• Confidence reflects scan quality and available inputs — when evidence is weak, the app says so.

TRACK AND REFLECT
• Monthly rescans show how each domain changed.
• A private, on-device journal per domain and month.
• Three voices to choose from: Analytical, Gentle, or Direct.
• Optional share cards for your results — palm photos are never included.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your device. Palm photos, birth details, journal entries, and reports are never uploaded.
• No account. No ads. No trackers. No analytics.
• Encrypted local storage; raw palm photos auto-delete within 24 hours by default (retention can be turned off entirely).
• Delete all data anytime in Settings — a full local wipe.
• The app's only network use is a one-time download of the open-source hand-detection model file.

WHAT PALMASTRO IS NOT
• Not medical advice — no diagnosis, no illness claims. For health concerns, talk to a professional.
• Not financial advice — no investment tips, no promises about money.
• Not deterministic — palm lines and star signs are reflective lenses, not verdicts.

PalmAstro is free, with no in-app purchases at launch.

Languages: English and Traditional Chinese.
Questions? support@palmastro.app
```

## Description — zh-Hant

```

看懂你的解讀
• 每個月的報告都會整理成一份簡單的指引：這個月值得多發揮什麼、需要多留意什麼。
• 「留意」是溫和的提醒，不是警告——App 不預言厄運，只是點出值得觀察的習慣。
• 每一條指引都能回溯到背後的訊號，你隨時知道它為什麼出現。

運作方式
• 依照引導完成七個角度的手掌掃描，即時回饋拍攝品質。
• 掌紋分析完全在裝置上進行，只產出分類型的線條特徵（清晰度、連續度、深淺）——不是生物辨識模板，也絕不上傳。
• 輸入生日（出生時間與地點可選填），星象訊號同樣在本機計算。
• 確定性的規則引擎為每個領域評分 0–100，附上等級與信心水準。

可解釋，不故弄玄虛
• 每個分數都有「這是怎麼算出來的？」頁面，逐項列出各訊號的貢獻。
• 信心水準反映掃描品質與可用資訊——證據不足時，App 會誠實告訴你。

追蹤與反思
• 每月重新掃描，看看每個領域的變化。
• 依領域與月份書寫的私密日記，只存在裝置上。
• 三種語氣任你選：理性分析、溫柔陪伴、直接坦率。

隱私至上

• 本機儲存全程加密；原始手掌照片預設 24 小時內自動刪除，也可完全關閉照片保留。
```
